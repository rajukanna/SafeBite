Culinary Substitution Playbook (For Cooking for Buddy)

🚫 Instead of Paneer:
1. Extra Firm Pan-Seared Tofu

Sear in 1 tsp oil with turmeric & salt. Absorbs palak, kadhai, and tomato gravies just like paneer.

2. Boiled Kabuli Chickpeas

Adds delicious creamy bite and high protein to gravies.

🚫 Instead of Curd / Dahi:
1. Cashew & Lemon Paste

Blend soaked cashews with lemon juice and a pinch of black salt. Gives identical velvety tang to marinades & gravies!

2. Coconut Milk & Tamarind

Rich, gentle creaminess for curries and stews with zero dairy.

🚫 Instead of Brinjal / Baingan:
1. Sliced Roasted Zucchini

Nearly identical texture and moisture absorption in sambar, curries, and stir-fries without nightshade issues.

2. Bottle Gourd (Lauki) & Mushrooms

Cooling Ayurvedic gourds that melt tenderly in dals and curries.
